What we do

Data sourcing and licensing.

Our work centers on identifying relevant data, understanding who can license it, and connecting that supply with organizations that have a defined need.

Data acquisition

02

Commercial licensing

03

Who we work with

Working with buyers and data owners.

Parralax operates between organizations seeking data and those authorized to license it.

AI labs and businesses

For teams seeking datasets, we begin with the intended application, data specifications, usage rights and practical delivery requirements.

Data owners

For organizations holding commercially valuable datasets, we explore potential licensing arrangements with interested buyers.

Any transaction depends on appropriate rights, source verification, applicable privacy requirements and agreed contractual terms. Dataset availability is not guaranteed.
