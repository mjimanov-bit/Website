00Data infrastructure for machine learning

The model is only as good as the data you can trust.

Parralax helps teams find, evaluate and prepare datasets for machine learning. We check what the data actually contains, where it came from, and whether it fits the problem, and we tell you plainly where it falls short.

02Approach

Start with the problem. Let the data earn its place.

A short, repeatable process that keeps decisions grounded in evidence.

Step 1

Understand the requirement

What will the model do, on what inputs, and how will success be measured? Vague data requests produce vague results, so we pin this down first.

Step 2

Find appropriate sources

We look for relevant data with licensing terms that match your intended use, and we record where each candidate came from.

Step 3

Evaluate quality and suitability

We inspect real samples and run measurable checks, rather than trusting a dataset's description.

Step 4

Report limitations openly

Every dataset has gaps. You receive a clear account of what the data can and cannot support, including when the answer is "this is not a good fit."

Step 5

Choose the simplest thing that works

If a filtered public dataset and a baseline model solve the problem, we say so. Complexity has to justify itself.

Principle

We don't promise outcomes we can't verify. Our findings are tied to the samples and checks we actually ran, and we document how, so your team can reproduce or challenge them.
