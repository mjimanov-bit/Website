What we do

Dataset development, sales and licensing.

We create and supply datasets for organizations working with artificial intelligence and other data-intensive technologies. Our work includes developing datasets to specification, acquiring existing data and arranging commercial licensing agreements.

01

Dataset development

We build datasets for specific technical requirements. This includes sourcing, organizing, cleaning and preparing data for machine learning training, evaluation and other applications.

02

Dataset sales

We supply datasets to AI laboratories, research teams and businesses. We work with buyers to understand their data requirements and provide suitable datasets under agreed commercial terms.

03

Data acquisition and licensing

We identify existing data sources and work with authorized rights holders to acquire or license datasets. Agreements define permitted uses, access rights, delivery requirements and commercial terms.

Who we work with

Working with buyers and data owners.

Parralax works with organizations that need datasets and those that hold the rights to commercially valuable data. We support both custom dataset requirements and transactions involving existing collections.

AI labs and businesses

For organizations developing AI systems, we provide access to datasets and develop new collections based on defined technical requirements. Projects begin with the intended application, data specifications, usage rights and delivery needs.

Data owners

For organizations holding valuable datasets, we explore opportunities to license or sell their data to interested buyers. We focus on establishing appropriate usage rights and clear commercial arrangements.

Dataset development, acquisition and licensing are subject to applicable rights, source verification, privacy requirements and agreed contractual terms. Dataset availability is not guaranteed.
