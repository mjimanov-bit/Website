What we sell

Data, not consulting.

Parralax provides access to datasets that ML companies can use for training, evaluation, research, and development.

  • Computer Vision Data
  • NLP Data
  • Image Data
  • Video Data
  • Text Data
  • Audio Data
  • Geospatial Data
  • Industry-Specific Data
  • Custom / Specialized Data

These are the dataset types the marketplace is intended to support. Not every category is currently available.

Why Parralax

ML teams often need specialized data that is difficult to locate or obtain directly. Parralax acts as the data supplier.

How it works

Where the data comes from.

Private companies and data owners may have valuable datasets that they are willing to commercialize. Parralax licenses those datasets from their owners and sells access to buyers.

  1. 01

    Source

    Find valuable datasets.

  2. 02

    License

    Obtain a license from the data owner covering the permitted uses.

  3. 03

    Prepare

    Organize the data and documentation.

  4. 04

    List

    Make the dataset available through Parralax.

  5. 05

    Sell

    ML companies purchase or access the dataset under the applicable terms.

The end product is data access. Datasets are offered subject to their applicable licensing terms.

For data owners

License your data to Parralax.

Parralax licenses datasets from companies and organizations, then sells access to ML companies. If you hold data that may have commercial value, you can license it to Parralax.

Unused or underused datasets can potentially become a commercial asset. Ownership, permitted uses, and compensation are determined by the applicable agreement.

License Your Data
  1. 01

    Describe

    Tell us what the dataset contains and where it came from.

  2. 02

    Evaluate

    We review the data and your rights to it.

  3. 03

    License

    Agree the license terms in writing.

  4. 04

    List

    Parralax prepares and lists the dataset.
